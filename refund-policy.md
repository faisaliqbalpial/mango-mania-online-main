# Refund & Cancellation Policy — আমের বাড়ি (Amerbari)

**Effective Date:** June 1, 2025  
**Website:** [www.ammerbari.com](https://www.ammerbari.com)

---

## 1. Overview

At আমের বাড়ি (Amerbari), we are committed to delivering the freshest Rajshahi mangoes to your door. Because our products are perishable agricultural goods, our refund and cancellation policy reflects the nature of fresh produce while ensuring fairness for every customer.

Please read this policy carefully before placing an order.

---

## 2. Our Right to Cancel Any Order

**আমের বাড়ি reserves the right to cancel any order at any time** — including after confirmation — for reasons such as:

- Stock unavailability or supply disruption
- Incorrect or erroneous pricing (technical or human error)
- Inability to verify delivery address or customer contact
- Suspected fraud or suspicious order activity
- Extreme weather, natural disaster, or force majeure events
- Any other circumstance at our reasonable discretion

We will notify you promptly if your order is cancelled by us.

---

## 3. Refund for Orders Cancelled by আমের বাড়ি

### 3.1 Pre-paid Orders (bKash / Nagad / Rocket / Bank Transfer)

If we cancel your order **after payment has been received**, you are entitled to a **full refund** of the amount paid.

| Step | Details |
|------|---------|
| Notification | We will inform you via SMS, WhatsApp, or phone call |
| Refund method | Same channel as your original payment |
| Processing time | Within **3–7 business days** |
| Fees | No deductions — full amount returned |

### 3.2 Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders

For COD orders cancelled by আমের বাড়ি **before delivery**, no payment will have been collected, so no refund is applicable.

If a COD order is cancelled **after the delivery agent has collected payment** (rare circumstance), a full cash refund will be arranged and coordinated with you directly.

---

## 4. Customer-Initiated Cancellations

### 4.1 Before Dispatch

- Contact us as soon as possible via phone or WhatsApp
- If your order has not yet been dispatched, we will cancel it and issue a **full refund** for pre-paid orders
- COD orders cancelled before dispatch incur no charge

### 4.2 After Dispatch

- Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may not be possible
- If a dispatched order is refused at delivery (COD), a **return delivery fee** may apply and will be communicated to you
- Pre-paid orders refused at delivery will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis; return delivery fees may be deducted from any applicable refund

---

## 5. Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Items

If you receive a product that is:

- Visibly damaged or spoiled upon delivery
- Significantly different from what was ordered

Please contact us **within 24 hours of delivery** with:

- Your order details (name, phone, order date)
- Clear photos of the issue

We will assess the situation and, at our discretion, offer one of the following resolutions:

- A **replacement** in the next available delivery
- A **partial or full refund** depending on the nature of the issue

> ⚠️ Claims made after 24 hours of delivery cannot be processed, as mangoes are perishable and quality cannot be assessed retrospectively.

---

## 6. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will **not** be issued in the following cases:

- The customer dislikes the taste or appearance (subjective preference)
- Minor natural variations in size, weight, or color
- Overripening due to improper storage by the customer after delivery
- Claims made more than 24 hours after delivery
- COD orders where the customer refuses delivery without a valid reason after dispatch
